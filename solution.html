PROPOSED SOLUTION

One digital home for India's heritage.

HeritageConnect brings discovery, storytelling, location, voice, scanning and multilingual access together in one platform.

🏛️
THE BIG IDEA

Discover. Learn. Preserve.

A modern platform that turns cultural heritage into an interactive digital experience.

SOLUTION PILLARS

How we solve the challenge.

01
🔎

Unified Discovery

Search for monuments, traditions, crafts, festivals, food, music, dance and cultural stories.

02
🎙️

Voice Interaction

Users can speak instead of typing, making the platform easier for different age groups and languages.

03
📍

Location Discovery

Explore cultural places near the user's location using interactive map-based discovery.

04
📷

Scan to Explore

QR and barcode scanning can connect physical heritage locations with their digital stories.

05
🌐

Multilingual Access

Support regional Indian languages together with international languages for global visitors.

06
🤝

Community Contribution

Encourage communities to share local stories, traditions and cultural knowledge.
