KEY CHALLENGES

Why does this matter?

🗺️

Information is scattered

Cultural information is spread across books, websites, communities and disconnected digital resources.

👥

Local knowledge is overlooked

Lesser-known traditions and local stories may not receive the same visibility as famous heritage sites.

🌐

Language barriers

Cultural knowledge should be accessible in regional and international languages.

📱

Digital engagement

Young people need interactive and mobile-friendly ways to experience cultural heritage.

WHO IS AFFECTED?

A challenge for everyone.

Cultural heritage belongs to communities, but discovering and sharing it can be difficult when information is inaccessible or fragmented.

Our project focuses on creating a bridge between cultural knowledge and modern digital experiences.

Our target users

  • Students
  • Teachers & Educators
  • Tourists & Travellers
  • Local Communities
  • Traditional Artisans
  • Researchers
  • International Visitors
