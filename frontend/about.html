OUR VISION

Making India's heritage discoverable.

India's cultural heritage is not limited to famous monuments. It lives in villages, streets, temples, homes, kitchens, workshops, classrooms, theatres and communities.

Every region has its own languages, food, clothing, music, dance, festivals, crafts, architecture, rituals and oral traditions.

HeritageConnect brings these different cultural dimensions together in one digital experience so that students, travellers, researchers and communities can explore them easily.

Our goal is simple: use technology to help culture remain visible, understandable and connected to future generations.

ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Frequently asked questions.

HeritageConnect is a digital cultural heritage platform designed to help users discover, learn about and connect with India's monuments, traditions, arts, festivals, crafts, food and stories.
Users can explore architectural heritage, classical and folk arts, festivals, traditional crafts, food, music, clothing, languages, rituals and local cultural stories.
India has a highly diverse linguistic landscape. Multilingual interfaces can make cultural information easier to access for people who are more comfortable using regional languages.
Technology can improve discovery, documentation, education and access. Digital stories, maps, photographs, multilingual content and QR experiences can connect physical heritage with digital learning.
The concept is intended for students, educators, travellers, communities, artisans, researchers and anyone interested in India's cultural heritage.
